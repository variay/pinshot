# PinShot

**Capture, annotate, pin on top.**

PinShot is a lightweight menu-bar screenshot tool for macOS. Capture a
region, window, or the full screen; mark it up with arrows, boxes, and
text; then pin the result as a floating always-on-top reference while
you work.

- No subscription. No account. No cloud.
- Fully sandboxed — your captures never leave your Mac.
