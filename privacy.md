# PinShot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 12, 2026_

PinShot does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any data. Period.

- **No data collection.** PinShot has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash
  reporting service, and no account system.
- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections of any kind.
  Your screenshots and recordings never leave your Mac.
- **Local settings only.** Preferences (hotkeys, save folder, options) are
  stored locally on your Mac in the standard user defaults database and in
  security-scoped bookmarks. They are never transmitted anywhere.
- **Your files stay yours.** Screenshots and recordings are written only to
  the folder you explicitly choose. PinShot can only access that folder,
  enforced by the macOS App Sandbox.
- **Screen Recording permission** is used solely to capture the screenshots
  and recordings you explicitly trigger. PinShot never captures anything in
  the background.
